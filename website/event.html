Core Event Mechanics & Quests

Mechanic #1 🛸

30-Min Drone Invasions

Drones patrol every 30 minutes in Abyss Ocean, Cosmic, and Angels & Demons. Whack drones with your bat to collect Samples (event currency) and chance for rare Drone Parts.

EXPERT WALKTHROUGH

Step-by-Step Quest Completion

STEP 1 Reach 2.5M Speed & Enter Abyss Ocean

Equip your highest-income Divine or Eternal pets to build up to 2,500,000 Speed. Head to Zone 6 (Abyss Ocean). Look for the giant luminous coral formation on the far-left reef wall.

Zone 6 Required
STEP 2 Interact with Nibbles (Experiment #013)

Walk directly through the translucent section of coral to enter Nibbles' secret laboratory. Press E to talk with Experiment #013 to receive the 5 Vault Parts questline.

Quest Unlocked
STEP 3 Retrieve All 5 Vault Parts

Part 1: Titan Temple behind the right stone pillar. Part 2: Cherry Blossom rooftop of the center pagoda. Parts 3, 4, and 5: Drop randomly from defeating Drones during the 30-minute invasion waves.

5/5 Parts
STEP 4 Claim The Scrambler Bat

Return to Nibbles with all 5 parts to crack the Stolen Vault and claim The Scrambler Bat (+450% Speed boost and scrambles opponent egg carry).

Weapon Claimed
