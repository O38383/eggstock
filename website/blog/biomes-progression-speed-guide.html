Early Game Strategy (Zones 1 to 5: Forest to Snow)

In the early zones, speed builds rapidly through basic pet hatching. Equip all unlocked pets regardless of rarity initially to scale your base money income. Use the Lake and Desert runways to safely practice evasion angles against the Scorpion guardian before taking on high-speed hazards.

Mid Game Scaling (Zones 6 to 8: Volcano, Abyss, Prehistoric)

The jump from 700K Speed in Volcano to 2.5M in Abyss Ocean is the game's first major progression gate. In Volcano, farm for Nightflame ($3.0B/s)—this pet provides the massive economy multiplier needed to power through Abyss Ocean and reach 18M Speed for Prehistoric.

Endgame Domination (Zones 9 to 12: Cosmic to Angels & Demons)

Once you cross the 700M Speed threshold in Cosmic, focus entirely on Divine pet breeding and mutations. Reaching 20 Billion Speed unlocks Zone 12 (Angels & Demons), granting access to the Secret Waterfall Fusion Shrine where the highest earners in the game—Equinox ($1.8B/s) and Aetheron ($6.9B/s)—are forged.
