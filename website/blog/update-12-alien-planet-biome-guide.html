Steal An Egg Update 12: Alien Planet Biome, 250B Speed & Lagoon Guide

By EggStock Research Unit • Updated November 12, 2026 • Verified Fandom Wiki Intel

Update 12 has officially arrived in Roblox Steal An Egg, introducing the highest-tier progression area in the game: the Alien Planet Biome. With a required gate threshold of 250 Billion Speed, the powerful Obsidian Rex guardian, the Alien Lagoon fishing sub-area accessed via the Alien Ferryman, and the coveted Divine Xenorath fusion, this guide covers every verified mechanic to help you master the new update.

1. Reaching 250 Billion Speed (The Entry Gateway)

The Alien Planet Biome represents Zone 14 in progression, positioned beyond the Enchanted Forest (50B Speed). You must reach at least 250,000,000,000 (250B) Speed on your treadmill to safely outrun the Obsidian Rex:

bolt FAST 250B SPEED CHECKLIST
  • Equip Top Fusion Pets: Fill pet slots with high-rebirth Equinox, Aetheron, or Celestial Sun Lion pets.
  • Saturday Admin Abuse (2x Luck): Take full advantage of the weekly developer event every Saturday @ 15:00 UTC for boosted speed progression.
  • Paranoid Pete Dialogue: Talk to Paranoid Pete in the lobby after crossing 250B to activate the teleport coordinates.

2. Alien Ferryman & Alien Lagoon (100 Fishing Milestones)

Once inside the Alien Planet, locate the Alien Ferryman NPC on the purple bioluminescent shoreline. Interacting with the Ferryman transports you across the gravitational channel to the Alien Lagoon:

phishing 100 FISHING MILESTONES

Fish in the luminous lagoon pools to earn milestone catches. Reaching milestones up to 100 awards upgraded rods, event currency, and Void Pearls.

water_drop DARK MATTER MUTATION

Collect Void Pearls from lagoon fishing to brew the high-multiplier Dark Matter Mutation, vastly multiplying pet income per second ($/s).

4. Halloween Event Status: Concluded & Removed

Please note that the Halloween Event (Update 10.5) officially ended in early November 2026. The Halloween Dimension portal (Graveyard, Haunted Swamp, Abandoned Manor), Candy Corn, and Ghost Fish are no longer active in the game. All event progression has transitioned into Update 12.
