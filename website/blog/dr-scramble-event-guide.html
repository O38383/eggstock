Dr. Scramble Event Guide: Drone Invasions, Nibbles & The Bat

By EggStock Research Unit • Updated October 23, 2026 • Roblox Steal An Egg Update 5

Update 5 introduced the game's largest event yet: Dr. Scramble's Mechanical Takeover. With sky drones swarming spawn worlds every 30 minutes, an underwater research facility hidden behind coral reefs, and the coveted Scrambler Bat weapon waiting to be assembled, this comprehensive guide covers every mechanic you need to maximize your rewards before the event concludes.

1. The 30-Minute Drone Invasion Cycle Explained

Every 30 minutes on the dot, sirens sound across all 16 biomes. Dr. Scramble dispatches a squadron of 12 flying surveillance drones that hover above egg drop pads for precisely 5 minutes.

schedule DRONE INVASION TELEMETRY & RULES
  • Cycle Interval: Triggers at :00 and :30 of every real-world hour.
  • Active Window: 5 minutes before drones self-destruct and despawn.
  • Drop Modification: Any egg that spawns in an active Drone zone receives a 3x Scramble Multiplier and drops 15–50 Scramble Scrap when stolen.
  • Danger Mechanic: Drones fire tracking laser beams that stun running players for 2.5 seconds. Dodge laterally or use terrain cover to break targeting lock.

You can track the live countdown to the next Drone Invasion anytime directly on our dedicated Event Hub.

2. Locating Nibbles' Secret Underwater Laboratory

Nibbles is Dr. Scramble's rebellious robotic assistant who has established a rogue research outpost to help players sabotage his creator's machinery. You must visit Nibbles to hand in Scrap and receive your daily event missions.

EXACT LOCATION — ZONE 6 (ATLANTIS / CORAL REEF):
  1. Enter Zone 6 (Ocean Trench) — requires 250,000 Speed.
  2. Swim down to the ocean floor and locate the glowing giant purple brain coral on the eastern boundary.
  3. Behind the coral is a narrow air-pocket crevice. Swim directly through the shimmering blue barrier.
  4. You will enter the pressurized glass dome containing NPC Nibbles and the Scramble Assembler Bench.

3. How to Assemble The Scrambler Bat (Best PvP Weapon)

The Scrambler Bat is the event's premier reward. Unlike regular bat tools, the Scrambler Bat features:

  • Electrified Knockback: Launches rival egg thieves 3x further on hit.
  • Egg-Drop Stun: Guaranteed 40% chance to force the target to drop their carried egg immediately upon contact.
  • Passive Speed Boost: +25% Sprint velocity while held in hands.
Required Component Quantity Source & Acquisition Method
Scramble Scrap 500 Pieces Collect during Drone Invasions (15-50 per stolen egg)
Repurposed Drone Core 3 Cores Shoot down or sprint through drone impact craters
Titanium Grip Handle 1 Handle Purchase from Nibbles for 100M base Cash
Scrambled Overclock Chip 1 Chip Complete Nibbles' 5-day daily quest chain

4. Dr. Scramble's Revenge: Preparing for Phase 2

In-game telemetry indicates that Dr. Scramble's orbital fortress is currently charging toward 100% capacity. When Phase 2 launches, players will face a server-wide raid boss encounter where all 16 biomes unite.

Ensure your avatar has accumulated at least 500M Speed and completed all of Nibbles' prerequisite blueprints to qualify for the global raid leaderboard rewards.
