How to Farm Money Per Second (MPS) & Build Speed Fast

By EggStock Research Unit • Updated October 29, 2026 • Comprehensive Economy Guide

In Roblox Steal An Egg, two interdependent stats govern your progression: Speed (studs/sec) and Money Per Second (MPS). Without sufficient Speed, you cannot survive crossing deeper biome gates or evade boss agro cones. Without high MPS, you cannot purchase incubator slots, speed upgrades, or rebirth bonuses. This guide outlines the exact mathematical loop required to scale your economy exponentially.

1. The Core Economy Formula Explained

Unlike basic clicker games where money increases linearly, Steal An Egg computes your net income tick using compound multipliers:

Net MPS = (Base Pet Output) × (Rebirth Multiplier) × (Biome Aura) × (Event/Server Boost)

Many novice players make the mistake of spending all their cash exclusively on Speed upgrades while neglecting incubator expansions. Equipping 4 Common pets in Forest gives +8 MPS, while hatching a single Legendary Basilisk in Zone 4 (Desert) yields +450 MPS — a 56x income leap.

2. Phase-by-Phase Speedrun Blueprint

PHASE 1: THE FOREST RUSH 0 to 10,000 Speed

Do not buy speed potions. Fill all 3 default base incubator slots with Grassland and Forest eggs immediately. Accumulate $25,000 to unlock your first Rebirth, which awards a permanent 2.0x MPS multiplier.

PHASE 2: VOLCANO & CYBERNETIC 10,000 to 1,000,000 Speed

Bypass slow Common eggs entirely. Use EggStock's live radar to alert you when Rare and Mythic eggs drop in Volcano and Cybernetic biomes. Secure 3 Phoenix eggs to achieve +1,200x income scaling.

PHASE 3: ABYSS TO AETHER 1,000,000+ Speed

At this tier, all income stems from the Zone 12 Secret Waterfall Fusion Shrine. Merging Mythic and Divine eggs grants upwards of +45,000x MPS, allowing you to afford maxed speed tiers instantly.

3. Capitalizing on Admin 2x Luck Weekends

Every weekend, the game developers activate a global 2x Hatch & Drop Luck event. During this 48-hour window:

  • Secret egg drop chances increase from 0.05% to 0.10%.
  • Incubator mutation chances double (Gold mutation adds +3x base MPS; Rainbow mutation adds +10x base MPS).
  • Always save your high-value Divine eggs in inventory until the 2x luck modifier is confirmed live in the server banner.
