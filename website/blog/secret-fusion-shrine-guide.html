Secret Waterfall Fusion Shrine: How to Fuse Equinox & Aetheron

By EggStock Research Unit • Updated November 7, 2026 • Roblox Steal An Egg v5.2

Hidden deep within the highest peak of Zone 12 (Angels & Demons) lies the Secret Waterfall Fusion Shrine. While standard incubators hatch single pets from individual eggs, the Fusion Shrine allows endgame players to combine two fully hatched Divine-tier or Secret-tier eggs onto dual pedestals to forge mythical hybrids with game-breaking multiplier bonuses.

1. How to Find the Hidden Waterfall Entrance

The entrance is deliberately concealed behind an opaque cascading waterfall in Zone 12. Most players sprint past assuming it is solid terrain collision. Follow these exact coordinates:

location_on ZONE 12 (ANGELS & DEMONS) — NORTHWEST CLIFFSIDE
  • Requires minimum 20,000,000,000 (20B) Speed to pass the Zone 12 portal barrier.
  • Head directly northwest past the Archangel Altar toward the cascading violet waterfall.
  • Align your avatar with the central purple stream and sprint directly into the water — the terrain collision is non-solid.
  • You will fall into a crystal-lit subterranean cavern containing the Twin Pedestal Dais.

2. Understanding the Twin Pedestals (Solar vs Lunar)

The altar contains two glowing glyph circles on the ground: the Solar Pedestal (Yellow Rune) on the left, and the Lunar Pedestal (Violet Rune) on the right.

Placing eggs in the incorrect order will invert the primary stat scaling. The Solar Pedestal dictates the final creature's base Multiplier per Second (MPS), while the Lunar Pedestal dictates its Speed and Carry stat augmentations.

Pedestal Slot Rune Color Governing Pet Attribute Optimal Placement
Left Pedestal Solar Gold Cash Multiplier (MPS) Place your highest MPS multiplier pet here
Right Pedestal Lunar Violet Sprint Speed & Carrying Capacity Place your highest Speed/Utility pet here

4. Critical Fusion Warnings & Best Practices

  • Sacrifice is Irreversible: Both parent eggs placed upon the pedestals are consumed during the fusion process. Never fuse your only copy of a Divine egg if you do not have a secondary economy pet active.
  • Check Server Stability: Do not activate the shrine during server restarts or when server ping exceeds 250ms. If you disconnect during the 10-second channeling cutscene, the server rolls back to the pre-pedestal state.
  • Use EggStock Live Radar: Monitor the live spawn radar to replenish your rare eggs before burning them in the shrine.
